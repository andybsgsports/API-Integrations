# Code of Conduct

This is a private, internal repository maintained by Badger Sporting Goods.
Everyone with access — employees, contractors, and collaborators — is expected
to act professionally and in good faith.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful, direct, and constructive in reviews and discussion.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the business.
- Giving and gracefully accepting honest technical feedback.
- Assuming good intent and asking questions before assigning blame.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sharing confidential, proprietary, or credential material outside the
  authorized team.
- Deliberately introducing insecure or destructive changes.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
commit messages, and any related internal communication.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner at **andy@bsgsports.com**.
Reports will be handled discreetly and reviewed in good faith.
